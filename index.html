About the Laboratory
Prof. Joanna I. Sulkowska

The Interdisciplinary Laboratory of Biological Systems Modelling is led by Prof. Joanna I. Sulkowska at the Centre of New Technologies, University of Warsaw. We sit at the intersection of physics, mathematics, bioinformatics and biochemistry — united by a single fascination: the complex topology of proteins.

We develop multidimensional models for energy landscapes, build proteome-scale databases, and design drug candidates targeting knotted enzymes in disease. Our tools — AlphaKnot, KnotProt, Topoly and more — are used by researchers worldwide.

Research Programme
From protein topology to targeted therapeutics
All research topics →

A team of physicists, mathematicians, bioinformaticians and biochemists — united by the study of proteins with non-trivial topology.

01
Protein Folding & Energy Landscapes
How do knotted proteins fold reliably? We build multidimensional models of folding energy landscapes using coarse-grained Go-models and maximum entropy approaches.
Go-models AFM simulations Topological barriers
Explore →
02
Complex Topology: Knots, Lassos & Links
Classifying and quantifying non-trivial entanglements in proteins and RNA — from mathematical knot invariants to proteome-scale analysis with AlphaFold.
Knot invariants AlphaFold RNA topology
Explore →
03
Drug Design & Therapeutics
Rational design of inhibitors targeting knotted enzymes involved in cancer and infectious disease. Topology as a novel pharmacological handle.
Knotted enzymes Inhibitor design Cancer targets
Explore →
04
Structural Bioinformatics & Databases
Building proteome-scale databases and bioinformatics tools. Direct Coupling Analysis for structure prediction. Open science infrastructure for the global topology community.
