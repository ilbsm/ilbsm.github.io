Protein Topology
Databases & Servers 5
Proteome-scale resources for detecting and classifying non-trivial topology in proteins.
AlphaFold
AlphaKnot 2.0
Database & webserver for entanglement analysis in AlphaFold-predicted protein models. Detects knots, slipknots and knotoids at proteome scale.
DatabaseServerAlphaFold
alphaknot.cent.uw.edu.pl →
AlphaFold
AlphaLasso
Server and database of proteins with lasso-type motifs found in AlphaFold models. Classifies loop and lasso topology in 3D biopolymer structures.
DatabaseServerAlphaFold
alphalasso.cent.uw.edu.pl →
KnotProt 2.0
Comprehensive database of proteins with knots, slipknots and knotoids from the PDB. The original reference resource for knotted protein structures.
DatabasePDB
knotprot.cent.uw.edu.pl →
LassoProt
Server and database of proteins with lasso motifs — covalent loops whose tail threads through the loop, classified by piercing number.
DatabaseServer
lassoprot.cent.uw.edu.pl →
LinkProt
Database of proteins with topological links — pairs of closed loops that are topologically entangled in protein structures.
DatabaseLinks
linkprot.cent.uw.edu.pl →

Package
Topoly
Python package for analyzing topology of polymers — computes knot and link invariants (Alexander, Jones, HOMFLY) for protein and polymer chains.
PythonInvariants
